How this tool works

Enter a School ID, a display name, and choose how the passphrase is created. The page derives Master_Salt, Master_Verifier, and the AES-GCM-encrypted Name_IV/Name_Enc using the same PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (300,000 iterations) scheme index.html uses to verify sign-ins. Nothing is sent anywhere. The result works in either passphrase mode — generated or typed.
